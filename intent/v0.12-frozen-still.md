# v0.12 The still of the green moment

## Goal

The green outline of v0.10 works, but it creates its own mistake: the user reacts to the
outline and the buzz by pressing the shutter, and the press moves the phone. The still is
grabbed after that movement, so the picture is a little blurred or off the outline the user
just saw, and the strict detection on the still fails more often than the live one. v0.12
closes the gap: at the moment the outline turns green the camera view grabs the still right
away and keeps it for a short window. A shutter pressed inside that window, while the outline
is still green and the document has not moved, gets that steady picture instead of a fresh
one. Outside the window, or after the phone moved, nothing changes against v0.11.

The shutter stays the user's act: the app grabs a candidate early, but it never takes a
picture the user did not ask for, and a candidate that is not used is discarded with the
window (decision 2026-09-23, see below). Along with it the shutter fires on the press instead
of the release, which also shaves the release movement off captures without detection.

## Scope

In:
- Frozen still: on the tracker's transition to `found` the camera view grabs the still with
  `captureStill` (one full-resolution draw, the same as the shutter does) and remembers it with
  the tracker's corners of that moment and the time of the grab.
- Window: `FROZEN_STILL_MS` (600 ms). The still is released when the window expires, when
  the tracker loses the document, when the toggle is switched off, when the frame is laid out
  anew (rotation, resize) and when the camera closes. At most one frozen still exists, and
  only for the window, so the extra memory is one capture canvas for well under a second.
- Shutter: `takeStill` on the camera view hands the frozen still to the capture when it is
  younger than the window, the outline is still green and every current live corner is within
  `AGREE_FRACTION` of the frame width of the frozen corners (the tracker's own tolerance);
  otherwise it grabs a fresh still as before. Either way the frozen still is given up.
- The still, frozen or fresh, then takes the v0.10 path: strict detection from the static
  frame, auto-bake behind the busy overlay, the warning when a green outline ends in a miss.
- Detect toggle as a switch: the v0.10 icon button (green when on) read as "press for magic"
  on the device. It becomes an on/off switch (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`): a track that is
  green when on, with a knob that carries the magic-wand icon and slides to the right. Same
  place, same label, same session state. `switchButton` in `src/ui.ts`.
- Camera back button in the white default style (black arrow) instead of the dark one, so it
  matches the shutter and the switch knob.
- Shutter on `pointerdown` (primary button) instead of `click`. The click that follows the
  press finds the camera session closed and does nothing; keyboard activation still arrives
  as a click and works.

Out: automatic capture, a burst or rolling buffer of stills, a frozen still with the toggle
off, using the live result for the bake (still the v0.10 rule), a visible indication of which
still was used, persistence of anything.

## Behaviour

1. Live loop as in v0.10. When `onLiveResult` sees `found` go from false to true: vibration
   (v0.10) and `freezeStill`: `captureStill(session)`, corners = the tracker's smoothed corners
   of this run (equal to the run's quad at the moment of the find), `at = performance.now()`,
   and a timer that releases the still after `FROZEN_STILL_MS`. A failed grab (canvas memory)
   is logged and leaves no frozen still; the loop goes on.
2. Every later run with `found` false releases the frozen still. Runs with `found` true keep
   it; the tracker's corners keep being smoothed as before.
3. Shutter (`takePhoto` in the app shell) calls `takeStill()`:
   - frozen still present, `now - at <= FROZEN_STILL_MS`, `liveFound`, and
     `quadsAgree(liveCorners, frozenCorners, AGREE_FRACTION * frame.width)` → the frozen still
     is the capture; the timer is cleared and the reference dropped (the page owns the canvas).
   - otherwise → the frozen still is released and `captureStill(session)` is the capture.
   The static frame, the strict detection on the still, the bake and the notice follow v0.10
   unchanged.
4. The agreement check protects the user's framing: a deliberate move after the buzz (to fix
   the framing) shifts the live corners away from the frozen ones, and the fresh still shows
   what the user aimed at. The window bounds the age of the candidate for the case where the
   document stays put and the user takes their time; a long-held phone gets a fresh still like
   before.
5. Releasing means `releaseCanvas` (width 0) so the memory goes back at once, not with the
   garbage collector.
6. The press: the shutter button listens to `pointerdown` with `button === 0` and to `click`.
   The press takes the photo; the capture closes the camera view synchronously (before the
   bake awaits), so the click that follows sees no session and returns. A press whose grab
   fails leaves the session open, so the click retries once, which is harmless.

## Data model changes

None in `src/model.ts`. `src/camera-view.ts`: `FROZEN_STILL_MS`, the private `FrozenStill`
record (`image`, `corners`, `at`), `takeStill()`, `freezeStill`, `releaseFrozen`.
`src/detect-tracker.ts` exports `quadsAgree`. `src/app.ts`: `takePhoto` takes the still from
the camera view instead of calling `captureStill` itself. No stored data.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit test (jsdom, app shell, stubbed detection): three agreeing live hits grab exactly one
  still; a shutter 550 ms later creates the page from that canvas without a second grab and
  bakes once.
- Unit test: a shutter after the window grabs a fresh still and the frozen canvas has width 0.
- Unit test: live corners that drifted beyond the tolerance while the outline stayed green
  lead to a fresh still.
- Unit test: losing the outline, switching the toggle off and closing the camera each release
  the frozen still.
- Unit test: a `pointerdown` on the shutter takes the photo and the following `click` adds no
  second page.
- On the phone: reacting to the buzz as fast as one can, the captured page is sharp and
  rectified, and the warning after a green outline has become rare. Holding the phone still
  for two seconds after the buzz and then pressing gives the same result as before. Moving the
  phone to a different framing right after the buzz and pressing gives the new framing.
- Memory: on iOS, scanning twenty pages in a row with the outline turning green on each does
  not hit the canvas memory limit (the frozen still is released within the window).
