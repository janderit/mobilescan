# v0.9 Pinch zoom on the image

## Goal

Two fingers on the image magnify it, so the user can check whether text is legible, whether a
frame edge sits on the paper edge, or what a brightness setting does to faint pencil, without
sharing first. Up to v0.6 this worked by accident: the browser zoomed the whole page. The v0.6
follow-up that pinned the app box to the viewport (commit 6b385cb) removed that, and with it
the only way to look closely at a scan. v0.9 brings it back on purpose, limited to the image:
the button bars, headers and slider stay where they are and keep their size.

## Scope

In:
- Pinch zoom and pan on the captured view (the display mode), the crop/rotate view and the
  brightness/contrast view. Two fingers zoom around their midpoint and pan with it.
- One-finger pan while zoomed in wherever one finger has no other job: captured view and
  brightness/contrast view. In the crop/rotate view one finger always drags handles, the
  frame or the rotation; panning there needs two fingers.
- Double tap toggles between the fitted view and 3x centred on the tap.
- Zoom is view state only. It is never stored, never baked, never shared and resets whenever
  the view is left, the page is switched or a bake replaces the image.
- Loupes (v0.6) and zoom coexist: loupes are hidden once the zoomed view is itself as
  magnified as a loupe would be.

Out: zoom on the camera view (that would be digital zoom, a different feature), zoom on the
share sheet and popover, mouse wheel or trackpad zoom (mobile only), a zoom indicator or
reset button (double tap resets), any animation of the zoom (direct manipulation only),
browser page zoom (stays disabled; the app shell must not move).

## Behaviour

Model
- `ZoomState { scale, tx, ty }`: a similarity transform applied on top of the view's fitted
  transform, in CSS pixels of the stage. `scale` 1 with zero translation is the fitted view.
  The composed display transform is `zoom ∘ fitted`; everything that maps between image and
  stage coordinates (hit tests, handle positions, loupe transforms, the shade polygon) goes
  through the composed transform, so the existing drag code needs no zoom-specific branches.
- Range: `scale` from 1 to the lesser of 8 and the scale at which one image pixel covers
  2 device pixels (the loupe cap of v0.6; beyond it the view shows blur, not detail). Pinching
  out below 1 clamps at 1, no rubber band.
- Pan limits, applied after every change: on each axis the content must cover the stage; if
  the content is smaller than the stage on an axis it is centred on that axis. Content means
  the frame region in the captured and brightness/contrast views and the union of the image
  and the frame's bounding box in the crop/rotate view (the frame may stick out of the image
  after rotation and must stay reachable).

Gestures
- Pointer Events only, two tracked pointers; no `touch*` or Safari `gesture*` events. The
  stages get `touch-action: none` (the captured stage currently has `pan-y`, which is no
  longer needed since the shell cannot scroll). The viewport meta keeps `initial-scale=1`;
  `user-scalable=no` is not added because iOS ignores it and the stages prevent the browser
  gesture anyway.
- Pinch: from the two pointers' positions at the last event and now, the new state is the
  similarity that maps the old pair to the new pair (scale by the ratio of distances,
  translate so the midpoint follows), then clamped. Rotation of the pair is ignored.
- Pinch start ends any one-finger interaction: in the crop/rotate view a handle, body or
  rotation drag in progress is cancelled with its loupes and the pending frame keeps the
  position it had when the second finger landed (no revert; the user sees where it is). In
  the captured view a pending swipe is dropped.
- When one of the two fingers lifts, the remaining finger continues as a pan (captured and
  brightness/contrast view) or does nothing until it lifts (crop/rotate view). It never starts
  a handle drag or a page swipe.
- One-finger pan at `scale` > 1 in the captured view replaces the page swipe: swiping between
  pages works only in the fitted view. At `scale` 1 the v0.5 swipe rules are unchanged.
- Double tap: two taps within 300 ms and 24 px, each without movement. At `scale` 1 zoom to 3x
  (or the maximum, if lower) with the tapped image point kept under the finger; otherwise back
  to the fitted view. In the crop/rotate view taps do nothing today, so no conflict; the
  double tap is detected from `pointerup` times and never delays anything.
- No history entry, no busy overlay, nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion` to switch off.
- A tap on a button while zoomed works as usual; the bars are outside the stage.

Rendering
- During a gesture: the stage content (canvas plus SVG overlay in the crop/rotate view, the
  filtered canvas in the brightness/contrast view, the preview canvas in the captured view)
  is moved with a CSS `transform` on a wrapper element, so every `pointermove` costs a
  compositor update only. Handles and frame line widths would scale with the wrapper, so in
  the crop/rotate view the overlay is not wrapped: its geometry is recomputed from the
  composed transform per move, which is what `layout()` already does for the fitted view.
- On gesture end (`pointerup`/`pointercancel` of the last pointer, or the double tap): a
  crisp redraw. The stage canvas keeps its size (stage in device pixels, `MAX_DPR` 2) and is
  redrawn through the composed transform from the full-resolution image, so it shows only the
  visible part at full detail. The wrapper transform is reset to identity at the same time.
  The captured and brightness/contrast views draw from `capture.image` (the current page
  always holds its canvas), not from the downscaled preview, so text becomes readable instead
  of blurry. Memory does not grow with the zoom level.
- Zoom state survives a `ResizeObserver` layout (orientation change, browser chrome) by
  re-clamping; it does not survive leaving the view.

Interaction with other features
- Loupes: hidden while `scale` multiplied by the fitted view scale is at least the loupe
  scale (3x fitted, capped); below that they work as in v0.6, with the cluster placed in
  the stage centre as before.
- Slider (brightness/contrast): unchanged, below the stage, outside the zoomable area. The
  CSS filter chain applies to the redrawn canvas as before.
- Auto (v0.8) buttons, confirm, back, 90°: act on the frame or image as before; confirm and
  90° reset the zoom because the image or its base rotation changes. Back resets it with the
  view.
- Page switch (buttons or swipe in the fitted view) resets the zoom.

## Data model changes

None in `src/model.ts`. New module `src/zoom.ts` with the pure parts: `ZoomState`, the
pinch update from two pointer pairs, the clamp against content and stage bounds, the double
tap target, the composition with an affine view transform. `src/editor.ts`, `src/tone-view.ts`
and the captured stage in `src/app.ts` share one small gesture tracker (two pointers, double
tap) that emits zoom updates and gesture start/end.

No new icons.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit test: a pinch that doubles the finger distance around a midpoint doubles `scale` and
  keeps the image point under the midpoint fixed (within 1e-9 in stage coordinates).
- Unit test: pinching out below the fitted view clamps to `scale` 1 with zero translation;
  panning past the content edge clamps so the content still covers the stage.
- Unit test: composing the zoom with the fitted transform and mapping a frame corner gives the
  same stage point as mapping through the fitted transform and then applying the zoom.
- On the phone: zooming in on a scan in the captured view shows text at full capture detail
  after the fingers lift (not the 2048 px preview); a one-finger drag pans; a one-finger
  drag at `scale` 1 still switches pages; double tap toggles 3x and back.
- Crop/rotate view: zoomed in, a corner handle still hits within its 44 px target and the
  drag moves the corner by the finger's movement in image pixels divided by the composed
  scale (the corner stays under the finger); the loupes are absent at high zoom and present
  at low zoom.
- Brightness/contrast view: the slider keeps working while zoomed, the filter preview
  applies to the zoomed content.
- Zoom resets on back, confirm, 90°, page switch and after a bake; nothing about the zoom
  reaches the PDF (share output is bit-identical with and without a zoom in progress).
- The app shell never zooms or scrolls: bars, header and slider stay in place during a
  pinch anywhere on the stage, and a pinch on a button bar does nothing.
- Performance: per `pointermove` during a pinch no canvas drawing happens (verified with a
  counter in a dev build); the crisp redraw on release stays under 50 ms on a mid-range
  Android phone for a 16 MP page.
